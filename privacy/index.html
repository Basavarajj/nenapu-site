Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026 · Version 1.0

In short

  • What you share goes only to the people you choose: everyone you’re connected to, one group, or only you.
  • No ads. We never sell your information. No strangers, no follower counts, no algorithm choosing what you see.
  • A feeling is saved on a moment only if you pick one. Nothing you share is analysed to guess how you feel.
  • You can download your moments or delete your account whenever you like.

Nenapu is a social app for the people you actually know. This page explains what we collect, why, who sees it, and the choices you have.

Who we are

Nenapu is run by Basavaraj Katageri, an individual based in Bengaluru, India. For anything about your privacy or your data, write to legal@nenapu.me. For help using the app, write to support@nenapu.me.

What we collect

  • Your account. Name, username, email and password. Your password is handled by our sign-in provider and stored scrambled; we never see it. A photo and a short bio are optional. If you use Apple or Google to sign in, we receive the name and email they choose to share.
  • What you share. Moments (words, photos, video, voice notes), the date, a feeling if you pick one, a place if you add one, chapters, Space messages, comments, reactions and poll answers. What you post in Live is included.
  • Your people. Who you’re connected to, invites you send and receive, and who you’ve blocked or muted.
  • Contacts, only if you allow it. Used to show which of your contacts are already on Nenapu. It is matching only: the list is not kept and nobody is messaged.
  • Camera, photos and microphone. Asked for when you first use them. We receive only what you capture or choose. Nenapu never asks for your location; a place is only what you type or pick.
  • Your phone. A notification address so alerts can reach you, the app version, phone model and system version to fix problems, and your IP address in our server logs. We do not use advertising IDs.
  • Your settings and consent. Your preferences, and when you accepted this policy.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t show ads, and we don’t sell or rent your information.
  • We don’t use trackers, advertising tools or analytics from other companies inside the app.
  • We don’t look through your moments to build a profile of you, guess your mood, or decide what you see. Your feed is newest first.
  • We don’t show strangers to you, or you to strangers.

How we use your information

To run Nenapu for you: show your moments to the people you chose, send the notifications you’ve turned on, keep your account safe, fix problems, answer reports of abuse, and meet legal duties. We don’t use it for anything else.

Who can see what

  • Together reaches everyone you’re connected to. A Space reaches only its members. My Life is only you.
  • In a shared chapter, everyone in it sees what each person adds there. People you share with can screenshot or save what they see, so share with care.
  • Deleting a moment removes it everywhere you shared it. If you delete your account, shared chapters you started stay for the other people in them.
  • Private Moments are locked with Face ID or your passcode on your phone. The lock keeps out people who pick up your phone; it is not end-to-end encryption.
  • Blocking someone stops them seeing you or reaching you.

Companies that help us run Nenapu

They process data for us and may not use it for their own purposes.

  • Supabase: our database, file storage and sign-in.
  • Render: the servers that run the app.
  • Apple and Google: they deliver notifications to your phone, and they sign you in if you choose Apple or Google.
  • LocationIQ: looks up place names, only when you add a place to a moment.
  • Vercel: hosts our website, nenapu.me.

Some of these companies run servers outside India, including in the United States. We share your information with the authorities only when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Your moments stay until you delete them. A deleted moment waits in Recently deleted for 30 days, then is erased. If you delete your account, we remove your information within 30 days. Deactivating hides your profile and keeps your moments until you return. Server logs are kept only as long as needed for security and fixing problems.

How we protect it

Everything travels over an encrypted connection. Sign-in is protected by a lock after three wrong tries, and you can turn on two-step verification. Nenapu is a small, young service and has not had a formal security audit, and no service can promise perfect security. If something goes wrong that affects you, we will tell you.

Your choices and rights

  • See and download your moments in Settings, under Download your data.
  • Correct your details in Settings, under Your details.
  • Delete or deactivate your account in Settings. You can delete single moments any time.
  • Take back a permission (contacts, camera, photos, microphone, notifications) in your phone’s settings, any time.
  • Withdraw consent, ask a question, or nominate someone to act for you if you die or can’t: email legal@nenapu.me. We reply within 30 days.

If you’re in India (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023): Nenapu is the Data Fiduciary and you are the Data Principal. Our Grievance Officer is Basavaraj Katageri, legal@nenapu.me. If we haven’t resolved your complaint, you can take it to the Data Protection Board of India.

If you’re in the EU or UK: we use your information to provide the service you asked for, and you can complain to your local data protection authority. If you’re in California: we don’t sell or share your personal information.

Age

Nenapu is for people 18 and over. If we find an account belonging to someone younger, we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we’ll tell you in the app before it applies, and update the date above.

Contact

Privacy and your data: legal@nenapu.me. Help with the app: support@nenapu.me.
